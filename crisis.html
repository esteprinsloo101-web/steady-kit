If you are in crisis

If you are in emotional distress, feeling unsafe, or thinking about suicide or self-harm, leave the app and get help immediately.

South Africa

SADAG 0800 567 567

24-hour Suicide Crisis Helpline (South African Depression and Anxiety Group). See sadag.org for current numbers.

For a life-threatening emergency call local emergency services.

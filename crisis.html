If you are in crisis

If you are thinking of suicide or self-harm, or feel unsafe, please reach out to people trained for crisis support right away.

South Africa

SADAG 0800 567 567

24-hour suicide crisis helpline (South African Depression and Anxiety Group).

Also: local emergency services / ambulance / police as appropriate for your situation.

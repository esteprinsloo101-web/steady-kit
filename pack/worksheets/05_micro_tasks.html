Worksheet 5 — Micro-Task Checklist

Behavioural activation in tiny steps. One tick is a win. Rest still counts.

  • □ Drink a glass of water
  • □ Open a window / step outside briefly
  • □ Stretch shoulders and neck
  • □ Wash face or hands mindfully
  • □ Tidy one small surface
  • □ Send one short kind message
  • □ Play one calm or lift track
  • □ Four rounds of box breathing
  • □ Put on shoes (even if staying in)
  • □ Eat a small snack if you have not eaten

# Steady Kit — Adult guide (18+)

This pack is for **adults**. It is **not** therapy and not a parenting curriculum.

## What these tools are for

Days when mood is low, energy is flat, or thoughts feel sticky. The goal is **one small steadying action** — not fixing your whole life in an afternoon.

## How to use (gentle protocol)

1. **Safety first** — If you are in crisis, stop and use crisis contacts (SADAG / emergency / findahelpline). Do not rely on worksheets.
2. **Check in** — Rate mood 1–5. One honest sentence is enough.
3. **Pick one lane** — Grounding, breathing, urge surfing, kind self-talk, or one micro-task.
4. **Stop when done** — Overdoing “self-help” can become another pressure. Rest counts.
5. **Professional care** — If low mood persists, worsens, or affects safety, contact a licensed professional or local services.

## What not to expect

- Guaranteed outcomes  
- Diagnosis or clinical advice  
- A therapist in your pocket  

## Privacy tip

Paper worksheets can stay offline. The web journal stays in your browser only — avoid shared computers if notes are sensitive.

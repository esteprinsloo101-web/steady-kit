    Coping deck

    Short skills you can use when emotions feel loud. Practice tools — not treatment.

    Look around and name, slowly:

    1. 5 things you can see
    2. 4 things you can touch
    3. 3 things you can hear
    4. 2 things you can smell (or like the smell of)
    5. 1 thing you can taste (or recall tasting)

    No rush. If a sense is hard, skip it. You are orienting to the present — that is enough.

    Box breathing: inhale 4 · hold 4 · exhale 4 · hold 4. Four rounds. Stop anytime.

    Urge surfing (one-pager)

    Urges often rise, peak, and fall — like a wave. You do not have to act on the peak.

    1. Notice — name the urge without judging: “There is an urge to…”
    2. Locate — where in the body? Tight chest? Restless hands?
    3. Breathe — slow breaths while you watch the wave, not fight it.
    4. Ride — wait 10–20 minutes if safe to do so; intensity often softens.
    5. Choose — after the peak, pick a tiny caring action (water, stretch, message).

    If the urge involves harm to yourself or others, stop this exercise and use crisis help immediately.
